# Kirimly Privacy Policy / Kebijakan Privasi

_Last updated / Terakhir diperbarui: 25 November 2026_

## English

Kirimly transfers files and text directly between devices on your local network (Wi-Fi, a hotspot, or Nearby Connections over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct). It has no accounts, no servers and no analytics.

**What Kirimly processes**
- **Files and text you choose to send or receive.** They travel only between your devices. Received files are saved to `Downloads/Kirimly` on your phone.
- **Transfer history** (file name, size, type, direction, device name, time) is stored only on your phone. You can delete it at any time in History.
- **Settings** such as device name, theme, trusted devices and a random device ID (used so trusted devices can recognise each other) are stored only on your phone.
- **Crash logs**, if the app crashes, are stored only on your phone. They are sent only if you choose “Share log” in Settings.

**What Kirimly does not do**
- It does not collect, sell or share personal data with us or third parties.
- It does not use the internet, advertising or tracking.
- It does not read your location. Android requires location or “Nearby devices” permission to create a local hotspot and to find nearby devices; Kirimly uses it only for that.

**Third-party components.** Nearby Connections is provided by Google Play Services and runs on your device; Google’s own privacy policy applies to Play Services.

**Security.** Android ↔ Android transfers are end-to-end encrypted. Transfers to a web browser (Safari) use local, unencrypted HTTP; use them on networks you trust or on the phone’s own hotspot.

**Children.** Kirimly is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

**Contact.** Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/edulazycreator-beep/Kirimly/issues

## Bahasa Indonesia

Kirimly mengirim file dan teks langsung antarperangkat di jaringan lokal (Wi-Fi, hotspot, atau Nearby Connections lewat Bluetooth dan Wi-Fi Direct). Tidak ada akun, server, maupun analitik.

**Yang diproses Kirimly**
- **File dan teks yang kamu pilih untuk dikirim atau diterima.** Hanya berpindah antarperangkatmu. File yang diterima disimpan di `Downloads/Kirimly`.
- **Riwayat transfer** (nama file, ukuran, jenis, arah, nama perangkat, waktu) hanya disimpan di HP-mu dan bisa dihapus kapan saja.
- **Pengaturan** seperti nama perangkat, tema, perangkat tepercaya, dan ID perangkat acak hanya disimpan di HP-mu.
- **Log crash** hanya disimpan di HP-mu dan baru terkirim jika kamu memilih “Bagikan log”.

**Yang tidak dilakukan Kirimly**
- Tidak mengumpulkan, menjual, atau membagikan data pribadi kepada kami atau pihak ketiga.
- Tidak memakai internet, iklan, atau pelacakan.
- Tidak membaca lokasimu. Izin lokasi/“Perangkat terdekat” diwajibkan Android untuk membuat hotspot lokal dan menemukan perangkat terdekat; hanya dipakai untuk itu.

**Komponen pihak ketiga.** Nearby Connections disediakan Google Play Services dan berjalan di perangkatmu.

**Keamanan.** Transfer Android ↔ Android terenkripsi end-to-end. Transfer ke browser (Safari) memakai HTTP lokal tanpa enkripsi; gunakan di jaringan tepercaya atau hotspot HP sendiri.

**Kontak.** https://github.com/edulazycreator-beep/Kirimly/issues
